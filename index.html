Routinix Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE] Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Routinix", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Routinix mobile application for Android (the "App") and the related services at routinix.app (together, the "Services").

Routinix helps you build daily routines, block distracting apps and websites while a routine is active or after a daily limit is reached, and stay motivated through points, streaks, leaderboards and a moderated community feed.

You can read this policy at any time in the App under Settings > Legal > Privacy Policy, or on the sign-in screen. If you have questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Summary

  • We collect the account and routine information you give us so the App can work across sessions and devices.
  • The App uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect which app is open on your screen. Only if you turn on Website blocking does it also read the web address in supported browsers. This information is processed on your device only. It is not stored on our servers, shared or sold.
  • The App uses Usage Access to calculate your screen time on your device. Your app-usage history is not uploaded to our servers.
  • We explain each of these in the App and ask for your agreement before you turn them on.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the App contains no advertising.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the App or through our web page (see Section 10).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Email address and password, when you register with email. Your password is stored only as a secure hash. We use these to create and secure your account, sign you in, verify your email and reset your password.
  • Name, email address and account ID from your Google account, when you choose "Continue with Google". We use these to create your account and sign you in.
  • First name, full name and chosen avatar, during onboarding or when you change your name and avatar. We use these to personalise the App and to show your profile on leaderboards and in the community feed.
  • Date of birth or age, during onboarding, to personalise the App, apply age-appropriate settings and comply with the law.
  • Onboarding answers (your goals, intent and estimated hours of daily phone use), to tailor routines and suggestions.
  • Routines, schedules, categories and completions, to run your routines, reminders, points, streaks and statistics.
  • Apps you choose to block (the app's package name and app name), custom app groups and daily limits, to enforce your blocks on this device and restore them on your other devices.
  • Unblock difficulty, unlock requests and unlock history, to enforce the unblock rules you chose.
  • A trusted contact's email address, only if you add a trusted contact, so we can send that person a code to approve your unlock requests.
  • Posts, bookmarks, reports and "pings" in the motivation feed, to show and moderate community content.
  • Support requests (category, description, and your app version, device model and Android version), to answer and fix your request.
  • Referral code, when you enter or share one, to apply trial bonuses to you and the friend who referred you.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Installation ID, device name, device model and Android version, to register your device, keep your account secure and support you.
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), to send routine reminders and account notifications.
  • Time zone, to schedule routines and reset daily points at the right local time.
  • Session and security records (for example sign-in time and the app's user-agent string), to protect your account, let you see and sign out active sessions, and prevent abuse.
  • Points, ranks, streaks and in-app activity events, to run leaderboards, seasons, rewards and statistics.
  • Install referrer from Google Play, only to read a referral code included in a Routinix share link.
  • Subscription and purchase status from Google Play, to unlock Premium features and verify purchases.

2.3 Information processed only on your device

The following information is used by the App on your phone and is not sent to our servers:

  • Which app is in the foreground, read through the Accessibility Service, so the App can show its block screen when you open an app that is blocked by a routine or by a daily limit.
  • The web address in a supported browser's address bar (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Opera and DuckDuckGo), read through the Accessibility Service only after you turn on Website blocking and agree to the explanation shown in the App. The App compares only the website's name with your blocked-website list and, if you leave it on, a built-in list of adult sites. Page content is never read. When a site is blocked, the App goes back, opens a neutral page (google.com) and shows its block screen. Your blocked-website list is stored only on your device.
  • App usage time for the last 7 days and today, read through Usage Access, to show your screen-time statistics and enforce daily limits.
  • The list of launchable apps installed on your device, so you can choose which apps to block. Only the apps you select are saved to your account (Section 2.1).

The App does not read what you type, your messages, passwords or other content inside apps, and does not record your browsing history.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, phone number, contacts, photos or files, microphone or camera data, SMS or call logs, health data, biometric data, advertising ID, or payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

3. Android permissions and why we ask for them

We explain each sensitive permission inside the App before asking for it, and you can turn any of them off in Android Settings at any time.

3.1 Permissions you grant in Android Settings

  • Accessibility Service ("Routinix App Blocking"). Detects the app in the foreground to block the apps you chose during routines and after daily limits. If you turn on Website blocking, it also blocks websites on your list and adult sites. When something is blocked, it goes back or to the home screen and shows the block screen. If you turn it off, app, website and daily-limit blocking stop working.
  • Usage Access. Shows your daily and average screen time and enforces daily app limits. If you turn it off, screen-time statistics and daily limits are unavailable.
  • Battery optimisation exemption and, on Xiaomi/MIUI phones, the background pop-up permission. Keeps blocking working when the App is in the background. If you turn these off, blocking may stop when the system closes the App.
  • Notifications. Routine start and end reminders, streak reminders, friend activity and account notifications. You can also choose which reminders you get in Settings > Notifications.
  • Alarms and reminders. Delivers routine reminders at the exact time. If you turn it off, reminders are still sent but may be a few minutes late.

3.2 Permissions granted automatically at install

  • Internet and network state: to connect to our servers and detect when you are offline.
  • Run at startup and wake lock: to re-schedule reminders after your phone restarts and deliver notifications.
  • Google Play Billing, Google Play Install Referrer and Firebase Cloud Messaging: for purchases, referral links and push notifications.
  • Vibrate: for notification alerts.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, phone, SMS, fingerprint or face unlock, and it does not use device administrator access.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as follows:

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account, profile, routines and progress: while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: recoverable for 30 days after you request deletion, then permanently deleted.

  • Notification delivery records: up to 90 days.

  • Purchase and transaction records: as long as required by tax and accounting law.

Information processed only on your device (Section 2.3) stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
